how to play

  1. Press start. There are three rounds, each harder than the last, with more pads and busier rhythms.
  2. In each round, press play (or the space bar). You hear a one-bar count, then one bar of sound.
  3. Play it back on the pads, in the same order and the same rhythm. Tap them, or use the home row of your keyboard (A S D F G H).
  4. You have three lives for the whole game, shown at the bottom. A wrong note or a wrong rhythm costs one. After a slip, you can't lose another until you get a note right.
  5. Stuck? Press play again on your turn to hear the pattern again. It's free.
  6. Get all three rounds right to win. Run out of lives and it's game over. Each round brings a new sound bank and tempo.

The small icons:

  • Flash the pads while the pattern plays.
  • A click on the beat during a game.
  • Switch the sound bank, between games. On a phone, tap it to allow motion access, then shake to switch.
  • Download the last pattern as a MIDI file.
